# Google Business Profile checklist: Oishii Nori, Santa Cruz

For searches like "sushi in Laguna" or "ramen near me", the Maps results at the top of Google come from the Business Profile, not the website. Work through this in business.google.com, then repeat the "Weekly" section every week.

## One-time setup

1. **Name:** exactly `Oishii Nori`. Don't add keywords to the name; Google suspends listings for that.
2. **Categories:**
   - primary: **Sushi restaurant**
   - secondary: **Japanese restaurant**, **Ramen restaurant**, **Takeout Restaurant**, **Caterer**
   - add **Delivery Restaurant** only if delivery is a regular service (it is: every Santa Cruz barangay)
3. **Address:** Pedro Guevara Ave, Santa Cruz, Laguna 4009. Check that the pin matches the map on the website.
4. **Phone:** set the main number. Use the **same format** everywhere: website, Facebook, GrabFood, foodpanda. Send it to the developer so it goes into the site's schema.
5. **Hours:** match the hours set in the dashboard. Add holiday hours ahead of each holiday.
6. **Links:**
   - Website: `https://www.oishiinori.com/`
   - Menu: `https://www.oishiinori.com/our-menu`
   - Order online: `https://www.oishiinori.com/menu`
   - Reservations: `https://www.oishiinori.com/menu?reserve=1`
7. **Service area:** Santa Cruz, Laguna. Add other towns only if you actually deliver there.
8. **Description** (paste, max 750 characters):

   > Oishii Nori is a Filipino-owned Japanese restaurant on Pedro Guevara Ave in Santa Cruz, Laguna. We serve baked sushi trays, maki and torched maki rolls, sushi boats, ramen bowls like chasu, black garlic, spicy miso and tantanmen, plus takoyaki, bento, donburi and drip tea. Dine in, take out, or order online for delivery anywhere in Santa Cruz. Planning a party? We do sushi catering with sushi boats and party platters for birthdays, office meetings and family gatherings. Japanese food with a Filipino twist, made fresh for everyday cravings.

9. **Menu:** add the main items with prices under Menu (or upload a menu photo). Use the exact dish names from the website.
10. **Attributes:** dine-in, takeout, delivery, reservations, payment options (cash, GCash), and anything else that is true.
11. **Photos (first batch, 20+):**
    - logo
    - storefront from the street, so people can find you
    - interior
    - each sushi/ramen best seller
    - a sushi boat
    - the team

    Name the files descriptively before uploading (`baked-salmon-sushi-oishii-nori.jpg`, not `IMG_2041.jpg`).
12. **Reviews link:** in the profile, use **Ask for reviews** to copy the short review link, then send it to the developer. It can go on receipts and on `/review` as a QR code.

## Weekly (15 minutes)

- **Post 1 update:** a promo, a new dish, an event (like the kids' sushi class), or a sushi-boat catering photo. Add the Order or Book button.
- **Upload 3+ new photos** of real dishes and real customers (with permission).
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, within a few days. Mention the dish naturally ("Glad you liked the Black Garlic ramen!"). No copy-paste replies.
- **Ask every customer for a review**, not only the happy ones. Use the QR code or the short link on the receipt or at the counter. Asking only satisfied customers ("review gating") is against Google's policy.

## Never do

- Pay for reviews, offer discounts in exchange for reviews, or have staff and family post reviews.
- Put "Best Sushi Laguna" in the business name.
- Create a second listing for the same place.
